VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

Menopause and changing comfort

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

What may help

Let your partner describe what has changed in their own words. A change in comfort or interest should not be interpreted as rejection.

Something to ask

Would you like me to learn more, listen, or leave the topic for another time?

What to avoid

Do not assume everyone experiences the same changes or needs the same treatment.

Sharing is optional. This guide creates no obligation to discuss or participate.
