VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

When intimacy feels uncomfortable

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

What may help

Someone can stop an uncomfortable activity immediately. Respect their account of what they feel; if they want help preparing for care, offer practical support.

Something to ask

What would help you feel comfortable, including stopping completely?

What to avoid

Do not encourage pushing through pain or treat discomfort as a negotiation.

Sharing is optional. This guide creates no obligation to discuss or participate.
