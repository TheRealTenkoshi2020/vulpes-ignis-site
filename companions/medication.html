VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

Medication and possible sexual side effects

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

What may help

If someone wonders about medication, listen and support their choice to discuss it with their prescriber. A partner cannot establish the cause or safely decide what treatment to change.

Something to ask

Would it help if I gave you time or helped you prepare questions for an appointment?

What to avoid

Do not pressure someone to stop or change prescribed medication.

Sharing is optional. This guide creates no obligation to discuss or participate.
