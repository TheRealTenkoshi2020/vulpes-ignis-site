VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

Find your words — printable mini-workbook

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

A free, private-first preparation sequence for a manageable conversation.

Use a blank worksheet in the way that suits you. If you print or save it, the copy stays outside the site’s clearing controls. Your answers are not collected.

WORKSHEET 1 OF 6

An observation, not a verdict

Describe an experience without assigning a motive to another person.

What happened that I actually observed?

What assumption am I making?

How could I describe it more neutrally?

WORKSHEET 2 OF 6

Identify a need

A need can guide a request without making another person responsible for fulfilling it.

What matters to me here?

What part is within my control?

What kind of support might I ask for?

WORKSHEET 3 OF 6

Make one request

A request needs room for a genuine no. Keep it specific and manageable.

What is one action I could invite?

How will I ask whether now is a good time?

What will I do if the answer is no?

WORKSHEET 4 OF 6

A gentle opening

Prepare language in your own voice. A conversation need not happen today.

What subject do I want to discuss?

What is my opening sentence?

How will I make postponing acceptable?

WORKSHEET 5 OF 6

Practice a pause

A pause is useful when it can be respected. You do not have to return to an unsafe conversation.

What tells me I need a pause?

What sentence can I use?

What is an optional return point, if appropriate?

WORKSHEET 6 OF 6

Receive a no

A declined invitation does not require an explanation or negotiation.

What response would show respect?

How can I handle my feelings privately?

What other option is within my control?
