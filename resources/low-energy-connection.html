VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

Connection on low-energy days

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

A flexible, no-pressure worksheet collection for real-life capacity.

Print blank pages or use them privately. Printed and saved copies are outside site clearing. No answers are collected.

WORKSHEET 2 OF 6

Connection without obligation

Connection is an invitation, not an exchange. Choose a form that does not imply a later activity.
