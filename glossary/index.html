VULPES IGNIS · ADULT EDUCATION

Plain-language glossary

For adults 18+. General education, not individualized care.

Accommodation
An adjustment intended to make an environment or activity more accessible. Read more
Accountability
Acknowledging one's actions and following through on changes rather than demanding forgiveness. Read more
Affection
An expression of care or warmth that need not be sexual. Read more
Aftercare
An optional discussion or action about support following an experience. Read more
Arousal
A physical or subjective response that does not by itself establish willingness. Read more
Autonomy
The ability to make decisions about oneself without another person claiming entitlement. Read more
Body neutrality
An approach that makes room for the body without requiring positive judgments about appearance. Read more
Boundary
A limit a person sets around their participation, access or treatment. Read more
Capacity
The time, energy, attention or comfort available for a particular choice today. Read more
Clinical review
Review by a qualified professional in a relevant area; linking research does not establish that such review occurred. Read more
Consent
Voluntary, ongoing agreement to a specific interaction; it can change at any time. Read more
Contraception
Methods used to reduce the chance of pregnancy; options and suitability vary. Read more
Desire
Interest or wanting; it is not the same as bodily response or permission. Read more
Fantasy
An imagined scenario or idea that does not establish an intention to act. Read more
Intimacy
A sense or experience of closeness; it may be emotional, physical or otherwise defined by the people involved. Read more
Libido
A general term for sexual interest or drive, which can vary. Read more
Lubrication
The presence or use of a substance that reduces friction; comfort and appropriate product use matter. Read more
Menopause
A life stage associated with the end of menstrual periods; individual experiences vary. Read more
Negotiation
A discussion of preferences, limits and potential agreements, with room for disagreement. Read more
Observational research
Research examining associations without randomly assigning the exposure of interest; an association alone does not establish causation. Read more
Pause signal
A way to communicate a need to stop or pause; it must be understood and respected. Read more
Pelvic floor
Muscles and related structures supporting the pelvic region; individual concerns may require assessment. Read more
Performance pressure
Feeling that a particular outcome is expected or required. Read more
Randomized trial
A study that randomly assigns participants to groups; results still depend on design, sample and applicability. Read more
Reciprocity
Mutual consideration, rather than an obligation to return every action. Read more
Reflection
A voluntary opportunity to notice and describe personal thoughts or preferences. Read more
Repair
Actions that acknowledge harm or conflict and attempt to address it; another person need not accept them. Read more
Request
An invitation that another person is free to decline. Read more
Sensory preference
An individual preference about environmental or physical sensations. Read more
Sexual orientation
Language about patterns of attraction; a person may use a label, several labels or none. Read more
STI
Sexually transmitted infection; testing questions depend on the individual and should be discussed with a healthcare professional. Read more
Transition time
Time to prepare for, change or recover from an activity. Read more
